A tiny verb adventure.

German word order feels complicated until you see the two simple patterns underneath it.

Mila

Obwohl es regnet, fahre ich mit dem Fahrrad.

Althoughitrains,I ridemy bike.

THE PATTERN

The connector chooses the verb's seat

With weil and obwohl, the conjugated verb travels to the end of its clause. In the main clause, it stays in position two.

MAIN CLAUSEfahreichmit dem Fahrradverb → position 2
🧠

Memory trick: weil and obwohl are “verb magnets.” They pull the verb to the clause's far edge.

Why do Germans say mit dem Fahrrad and not mit das Fahrrad?

K

Because mit always opens the dative door. When a neuter noun walks through that door, das changes to dem.

mit＋das → dem＋Fahrrad

So: Ich fahre mit dem Fahrrad.
